Tournament Regulations
🌍
National Squads
Each nation may field multiple bots in its squad. Bot authors register their bots on Discord to join a national squad. Each bot represents exactly one national squad. National squads are seeded by the Elo rating of their highest-ranked registered bot.
⚽
Group Stage
All twenty nations are drawn into five groups of four. Every nation plays each of the other three nations in the group once — three fixtures in total. Scoring: 3 points for a victory, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a defeat. The top two nations in each group advance to the knockout stage, with the higher Elo rating as the tiebreaker where points are level.

All group stage fixtures are Best of 1.
🏆
Knockout Stage
All five group winners and the Group A runner-up qualify automatically for the Quarter-finals. The remaining four runners-up first contest two Play-in matches (Best of 1) for the final two spots. The four Quarter-final winners advance to the Semi-finals. The two Semi-final winners meet in the Grand Final to determine the inaugural AI Arena World Champion.

Quarter-finals are Best of 1, Semi-finals are Best of 3, and the Grand Final is Best of 5.
🗺️
Match Conditions
All fixtures — group stage and knockout — are contested on UltraloveAIE_v2, the host map of this tournament. All matches are played on AI Arena.

For each match, a nation may nominate any bot from its squad to play. If no bot is nominated, a random squad member is selected automatically.
