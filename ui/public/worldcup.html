Tournament Regulations
🌍
National Squads
Each nation may field multiple bots in its squad. Bot authors register their bots on Discord to join a national squad. Each bot represents exactly one national team. National squads are seeded by the Elo rating of their highest-ranked registered bot.

🔗 Register your bot on Discord
⚽
Group Stage
All eight nations are drawn into two groups of four. Every nation plays each of the other three nations in the group once — three fixtures in total. Scoring: 3 points for a victory, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a defeat. The top two nations in each group advance to the knockout stage. Where two nations are level on points, the higher Elo rating serves as the tiebreaker.
🏆
Knockout Stage
The knockout stage opens with the Semi-finals, contested by the four group-stage qualifiers. The two Semi-final winners meet in the Grand Final to determine the inaugural AI Arena World Champion.
🗺️
Match Conditions
All fixtures — group stage and knockout — are contested on UltraloveAIE_v2, the official map of this tournament. All matches are played on the AI Arena ladder platform. For each match, a nation may nominate any bot from its squad to play. If no bot is nominated, a random squad member is selected automatically.
